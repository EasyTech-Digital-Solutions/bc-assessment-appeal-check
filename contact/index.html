Contact PropertyTaxBC

Questions, corrections or feedback about the site.

Email info@propertytaxbc.ca. We read every message and usually reply within a few business days.

Good reasons to write

  • Something on a page looks wrong or out of date.
  • A calculator gave a result that doesn't match your notice or tax bill.
  • A link is broken.
  • Privacy questions about this site.

What we can't help with

We can't look up your property, review your assessment, file a complaint for you or give tax or legal advice. For your own assessment, contact BC Assessment. For the home owner grant or deferment, contact the Province of BC or your municipality.
