Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

EasyTech Digital Solutions ("we," "us," "our") operates this website to help British Columbia homeowners understand their property assessment and property tax deferment options, and, where appropriate, connect them with independent property tax professionals. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how it's handled, in accordance with BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What we collect

No account is required to use either tool. The property tax deferment eligibility checker keeps the values you enter in your browser — there is no form to submit on that page, and it has no connection to the referral program. The one exception: when you get a result, we log that a check was completed and which general outcome it fell into (for example, "eligible" or "not eligible"), with no answers, address, or other details attached. This anonymous signal is sent via Google Analytics — see "Cookies and analytics" below.

The free assessment check tool works the same way for the values you enter — they stay in your browser and are never sent to us or stored anywhere unless you choose to submit the referral form described below. The one exception: when you get a result, we log that a check was completed and which general category it fell into (for example, "worth investigating" or "roughly in line"), with no dollar figures, address, or other details attached, so we can see whether the tool is actually being used. This anonymous signal is sent via Google Analytics — see "Cookies and analytics" below.

When you submit the referral form on the free assessment check page, we collect:

  • Your name and contact information (email, phone)
  • Your property address and/or assessment roll number
  • Your current and previous assessed values
  • A description of your situation, as you provide it

Why we collect it

We collect this information for one purpose: to determine whether your situation looks worth pursuing, and, if you choose to proceed, to introduce you to an independent BC property tax professional who may be able to help.

Who we share it with

If you submit the referral form, your information is shared with the property tax professional(s) currently participating in our referral program, so they can contact you directly about a possible consultation. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone beyond this specific purpose without your separate consent.

Consent

By checking the consent box and submitting the referral form, you agree to this collection, use, and disclosure. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (see below), though this may mean we're no longer able to refer you.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and only retain it for as long as needed to fulfill the purpose above. Referral form submissions are stored using Cloudflare Workers KV, a secured storage service, and are not publicly accessible. Access is limited to those operating this site.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information (where we're not required to keep it)

Contact us at info@propertytaxbc.ca for any of the above, or with questions or complaints about how your information is handled. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the BC Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner (oipc.bc.ca).

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand general visitor traffic — which pages people visit, roughly where from, and how they found the site. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this and may combine this data with information from other sites you've visited, under Google's own privacy policy. It does not include the personal information you submit through the referral form. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.
